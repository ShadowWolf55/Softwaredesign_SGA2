An exploration of how metallurgy, chemistry, and mechanical engineering transformed small arms from 13th-century hand cannons into modern precision instruments.

Historical Milestones

14th – 17th Century

Matchlock & Flintlock

Early ignition systems relied on burning cords and striking flint against steel frizzens to ignite black powder, revolutionizing infantry tactics across Europe and Asia.

19th Century

Metallic Cartridges

The invention of self-contained brass cartridges with integrated primers eliminated loose powder and enabled reliable breech-loading and repeating firearms.

20th Century – Present

Smokeless Powder & Semi-Autos

Nitrocellulose-based propellants increased muzzle velocities while reducing fouling, paving the way for recoil- and gas-operated repeating actions.
